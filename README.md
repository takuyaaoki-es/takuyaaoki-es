# Takuya Aoki

Independent researcher and developer working on AI knowledge architecture and inter-intelligence representation.

I develop **VATES**, a B2B AI knowledge infrastructure system, alongside independent research on knowledge representation, interpretation, observer-relative knowability, and heterogeneous intelligences.

## Published Research

### IIIS: An Inter-Intelligence Intermediary Syntax for Shared Observation Across Perceptions

An intermediary syntax that lets intelligences with differing perceptions observe one and the same knowledge structure.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21374493

### The Abstraction Fixation Mismatch Hypothesis

What changes when an abstraction usable as an optional aid is fixed as a structure that subsequent processing must follow.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22742252

### The Structure-Observer Interpretation Hypothesis

Interpretation as a function of Structure and Observer Intelligence:

**I = f(S, O)**

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22792612

### The Observer-Relative Knowability Hypothesis

Knowability as relative to the observer and its epistemic capability level:

**K = K(O, L)**

Inaccessibility to one observer does not establish universal unknowability.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22822103

### The External Axiomatic Requirement Hypothesis

Extends SOIH and ORKH to their theoretical totalities S* and O*, and argues that grounding their existence non-circularly requires positing an external axiomatic position A from necessity.

**A = X**

Its content remains unspecified.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22855120

### An Evaluative Reference Model for Interpretation Formation Across Heterogeneous Intelligences

An experimental reference model for analyzing differences in the evaluative structures through which humans and AI form interpretations from the same facts.

The concept of evaluative reference underlying ERM has informed VATES's design decisions from the outset; ERM itself is not implemented as an executable function within VATES.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22911471

### An Epistemic Stage for Multiple Observer Intelligences

Positions SOIH, ORKH, and EARH together as a single epistemic stage in which humans, AI, and other possible intelligences can be treated as Observer Intelligence O, while preserving compatibility with human-centered epistemology.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22986516

### Structural Principles of Inter-Intelligence Intermediary Syntax (SP-IIIS)

Clarifies the structural principles of IIIS: shared reference, observer-relative representation, and structured reinterpretability across different Observer Intelligences.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23116534

## Implementation

### VATES

VATES is AI knowledge infrastructure for organizations that need reliable answers from information they control.

It keeps knowledge structured, human-readable, directly editable, and traceable to its source rather than turning organizational knowledge into opaque vector embeddings.

VATES can be used as a standalone conversational interface, embedded into an existing website, or integrated into products and AI workflows through its API.

**Website:** https://vates.standout.jp/
